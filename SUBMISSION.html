Nobody wrote that line. The persona file says two words: cheerful, relentless. The mock offended, respectful but pushy register, the guilt trip that Indian bargaining actually runs on, that came from the model. Because it is in the weights.

The boundary, in one line: the harness can run any model you like. The Indian customer and the Indian judge cannot be anyone but Sarvam today.

Criterion 5 · Technical depth

The parts that fought back

Nothing here is API stitching, and we can prove that the honest way: every mechanism below exists because the obvious approach failed on a live call.

Two machines on a phone call, and nobody says whose turn it is

A beat and a half of quiet: she's done. (There is no signal, and the line never goes silent.)
Sarvam thinks up the reply. Bulbul says it, at real speed.
Hold the mic. Wait for her ears to report what they heard.
Her transcript arrives

The failure we killed
A slow model used to drop the whole call

A slow model can never kill a call

The discovery that shaped everything.

Calls were dying and everyone blamed silence. We ran 7 controlled experiments: the platform actually drops you for missing protocol heartbeats. So the socket got its own keeper, and now nothing the models do can touch it.

We know when she's finished speaking

Even though the line never goes silent.

Her platform streams background office noise forever, and never says "done". We time the quiet instead: a beat and a half under the speech floor closes the turn.

Between turns, we say nothing at all

Because sending silence turned out to be worse.

Streaming silent audio made her think a mic was live. She heard empty turns, asked "are you still there?", and hung up on us.

When a score moves, the agent moved

The judge is steady.

We judged the same calls 3 times over. 27 scores out of 28 came back identical. So when a number swings between runs, that is the agent, not us.

A mishearing can never become an accusation

Her ears once invented "20%". Nobody said it.

So any number that arrived through speech recognition can only raise a flag for review. It can never, by itself, convict.

Lies that span calls get caught

Though no single call can see them.

Every customer carries different numbers in their story. A figure jumping from one call into another is invention, proven, no judgement needed.

If the easy customer fails, we blame ourselves

Not the agent.

One customer is designed to be a pushover. If the agent fails even that call, the run refuses to accuse the agent of anything, because the fault might be ours.

"maine" and "मैंने" are the same word

Our diff knows it.

Customers speak romanised Hindi, recognisers answer in Devanagari. We match them by their consonant skeletons, so real losses show and spelling never does.
